Do's and Don'ts

Do

  • Reuse --token-primary / --token-secondary for any new accent.
  • Keep new UI text in JetBrains Mono unless it's the app title.
  • Pair any saturated-accent state with a non-color signal.
  • Keep floating elements flat; reserve the floating-readout shadow for detached elements.

Don't

  • Don't introduce a third brand color.
  • Don't reach for --accent, --sans, --code-bg, --social-bg (unused scaffold leftovers).
  • Don't add resting-state shadows/gradients "to look nice."
  • Don't add decorative motion that doesn't encode simulated data.
